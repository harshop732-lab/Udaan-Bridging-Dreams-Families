Executive Summary

Udaan is a social-impact prototype designed to support children living in orphanages, child-care institutions, schools, and trusts.

The initiative goes beyond basic donations by building emotional support, creative confidence, life skills, career awareness, and long-term mentorship. It creates a responsible referral bridge between interested prospective adoptive parents and legally authorised adoption agencies.

Mentorship

Providing stable, trusted adult mentors for individual encouragement.

Creativity

Opportunities in dance, poetry, storytelling, art, and self-expression.

Guidance

Awareness of careers, education pathways, and employability skills.

Bridging

Coordination among NGOs, volunteers, and legal adoption agencies.

Vision, Mission & Objectives

Our Vision

A future where every child in institutional care is heard, supported, skilled, and able to pursue a meaningful life path.

Our Mission

To create safe, structured partnerships that provide creativity, mentorship, aspiration mapping, career guidance, and need-based support to children.

Four-Pillar Solution Model

A structured approach to transforming institutional care into guided pathways of opportunity.

1

Skill & Creativity Hubs

Weekly sessions helping children explore talents safely through dance, poetry, art, and digital literacy.

  • Yoga & Theatre
  • Creative Writing
  • Digital Literacy
2

Dream Mapping & Mentorship

Supporting each child to identify interests, strengths, and career paths without forcing decisions.

  • Dream Journals
  • Mentor Matching
  • Career Days
3

Needs-to-Support Network

Capturing verified institutional needs and connecting them with donors and CSR teams transparently.

  • Needs Assessment
  • Priority Boards
  • Impact Updates
4

Referral Bridge

Educating prospective adoptive parents and referring them only to officially authorised adoption channels.

  • Legal Awareness
  • Authorised Referrals
  • No Informal Matching
