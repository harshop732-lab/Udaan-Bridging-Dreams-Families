Udaan is a child-centred development ecosystem — not just a donation platform, not an adoption intermediary. We build emotional support, creative confidence, life skills, and long-term mentorship for children in institutional care.

Explore the four pillars → Mumbai pilot

01 — The gap

Children in care have shelter, food, schooling — but often lack consistent mentoring, creative expression, and a trusted adult.

Many institutionalised children struggle to express aspirations, identify strengths, or plan for life after care. The system has good intentions but fragmented support.

"Udaan aims to turn care into opportunity and aspiration into a guided pathway."

🧠

Emotional support gap

Limited access to stable, trusted adult mentors and individual encouragement.

🎨

Creative exposure gap

Insufficient opportunities in dance, poetry, art, music, and self‑expression.

📚

Career guidance gap

Limited awareness of careers, scholarships, and employability skills.

⚖️

Adoption‑awareness gap

Prospective parents may not understand the authorised, child‑centred process.

04 — Prototype features

Tools that give voice, track progress, ensure safety.

📔

Dream Journal

Paper or secure digital record where children draw, write, or speak about goals.

🛂

Skill Passport

Tracks workshop participation, interests, certificates, and achievements.

📊

Mentor Dashboard

Secure volunteer tracking for sessions, notes, training status, and escalation.

📋

Needs Board

Institution-approved list of items, services, and programme needs.

🃏

Career Path Cards

Simple guides for nurse, dancer, teacher, designer, entrepreneur, engineer, chef.

⚖️

Adoption Info Page

Explains legal process, directs users to authorised agencies. Reduces misinformation.

🛡️

Safeguarding is non‑negotiable

Background checks, child‑safeguarding orientation, no private contact, no public sharing of identities. Clear grievance and escalation routes. We refer only to authorised legal channels for adoption.

Police verification Boundaries training Consent protocols
