Every child's dream, every family's hope.

Udaan helps children in orphanages, child-care institutions, schools and trusts find their voice, build skills and meet mentors, and it points interested families only to legal adoption channels.

Shelter and schooling are not the whole story.

Many children in care have food, a bed and classes, but little consistent mentoring, creative expression, career exposure or one-to-one attention. Some struggle to say what they hope for or plan for life after care. Six gaps stand in the way.

Vision

A future where every child in institutional care is heard, supported, skilled, and able to pursue a meaningful life path.

Mission

Create safe, structured partnerships that provide creativity, mentorship, aspiration mapping, career guidance and need-based support to children.

Primary beneficiaries

Children in partner child-care institutions, orphanages, schools and trusts.

Secondary beneficiaries

Partner NGOs, volunteers, mentors, educators, counsellors, donors, and prospective adoptive parents seeking correct legal guidance.
